US city coordinates in us-cities.json are derived from the U.S. Cities Database
by Kelvin S. do Prado (https://github.com/kelvins/US-Cities-Database), used under
the MIT License. Puerto Rico rows were omitted. Coordinates are rounded to
4 decimal places and duplicate city/state names were collapsed.
