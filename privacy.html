1. Who we are

Boxing Buddies ("Boxing Buddies," "we," "us," or "our") is a free web app, available at https://boxingbuddies.vercel.app and installable as a progressive web app (PWA), that helps amateur boxers in the United States find sparring partners nearby.

Boxing Buddies is operated by Cyrus Justin, an individual (operating as Boxing Buddies). You can reach us at cyrusjustin06@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Boxing Buddies website, the installable app, and related services (together, the "Service"). Please read it together with our Terms of Service.

2. The short version

  • We collect what we need to run a sparring-partner matching app: your account login, your boxing profile, your approximate location, your swipes and matches, your messages, and sparring sessions you set up.
  • Other users see your profile and your approximate distance from them. They do not see your exact GPS coordinates.
  • We do not sell your personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not take payments.
  • We use a small number of service providers (Supabase, Vercel, Google sign-in, Google Fonts, jsDelivr, and OpenStreetMap Nominatim) to run the Service.
  • You can delete your account, and you can ask us to delete your data, at any time.
  • Boxing Buddies is only for people 18 and older.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you give us

Account information. When you sign up, you sign in either with an email "magic link" or with your Google account. We receive your email address and a unique account identifier. If you use Google sign-in, Google shares basic profile information with us (typically your email address, and may include your name and profile picture). We never receive your Google password.

Profile information. To create your boxing profile you may provide:

  • Display name
  • Profile photo
  • Town or general area, and your location (see Section 3.2)
  • Weight class
  • Experience level
  • Stance (for example, orthodox or southpaw)
  • Preferred sparring intensity
  • Glove preferences
  • Days you are available
  • A short bio

Activity in the app. When you use the Service, we store:

  • Your swipes on other profiles (for example, "spar" or "pass")
  • Your matches (when two users both choose to spar)
  • Chat messages you send to and receive from your matches
  • Sparring sessions you propose or respond to, including the gym, date and time, and status (for example, proposed, accepted, declined, or cancelled)

Reports and blocks. If you report or block another user, we store the report or block, who it concerns, and any reason or details you provide. If another user reports you, we store that report too.

Gym submissions. If you submit a gym, we store the gym's name, address, and website, and the fact that you submitted it. Once a gym is confirmed or approved, the gym listing (but not your identity as the submitter, unless we tell you otherwise) becomes publicly visible to users of the Service.

Gym listing data comes partly from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, and is available under the Open Database License.

Communications with us. If you contact us, we keep your message and our reply.

3.2 Location information

Location is central to how Boxing Buddies works, because it lets us show you sparring partners nearby.

  • GPS location (optional). If you allow it in your browser or device, we receive your device's latitude and longitude at the time you set or update your location. We store only the rounded point described below, not the more precise reading.
  • Typed location. Instead of GPS, you can type a city or ZIP code. We convert ("geocode") it into an approximate latitude and longitude.
  • Geocoding. To convert a typed city or ZIP code into coordinates, or coordinates into a town name, we send that location text or those coordinates to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service (see Section 6).
  • What we store. We store your town or approximate area and a latitude/longitude point that is rounded to about one kilometer (two decimal degrees). We do not keep the more precise reading from your device.
  • What other users see. Other users see your town or general area and your approximate distance in miles. They cannot read your latitude or longitude. The app asks the database for the distance only.

We do not continuously track your location in the background. Your location is updated only when you set or refresh it. You can change your location to a typed city or ZIP code at any time, or turn off location permission in your browser or device settings.

3.3 Information collected automatically

Like most web services, our hosting and database providers automatically process certain technical information when you use the Service, such as your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, pages or features requested, date and time of requests, and error logs. This is used to deliver the Service, keep it secure, and fix problems.

Cookies and local storage. We use cookies, browser local storage, and similar technologies that are necessary to keep you signed in, remember your settings, and let the installable app work. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking for advertising. We do not currently use analytics tools. If we add one, we will update this policy.

4. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide the Service: create and manage your account, show your profile to other users, calculate approximate distances, suggest nearby sparring partners, create matches, deliver chat messages, and let you propose and manage sparring sessions.
  • Show gym listings: display gyms and let users suggest new ones.
  • Keep the community safe: review reports, enforce blocks, investigate suspected violations of our Terms of Service, and remove accounts or content when needed.
  • Secure and maintain the Service: prevent fraud, spam, and abuse; debug and fix problems; and protect our systems.
  • Communicate with you: send sign-in links, important notices about your account, and changes to our policies, and respond to your questions.
  • Comply with law: meet legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, and establish or defend legal claims.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

5. What other users can see

When you have a profile on Boxing Buddies:

  • Other signed-in users can see your display name, profile photo, town or general area, approximate distance from them, weight class, experience, stance, intensity, glove preferences, availability days, and bio.
  • Your matches can also see the chat messages you exchange with them and the sparring sessions you propose to them or accept from them (including the gym and time).
  • Other users cannot see your email address, your exact latitude and longitude, your swipes (including who you passed on), or whom you have reported or blocked.

Please think carefully about what you put in your profile, bio, photo, and messages. Anything you share with another user can be copied or shared by them, and we cannot control what they do with it.

6. Who we share information with

We share personal information only as described below.

Other users. As described in Section 5.

Service providers (processors). We use trusted providers to run the Service. They process information on our behalf and only as needed to provide their services:

  • Supabase – database, user authentication (including email magic links and Google sign-in), and file storage (including profile photos). Supabase stores the account, profile, activity, message, and photo data described in this policy.
  • Vercel – website and app hosting and content delivery. Vercel processes technical data such as IP addresses and request logs when you load the Service.
  • Google – if you choose "Sign in with Google," Google authenticates you and shares basic account information with us. Google's use of your information is governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim – geocoding. When you type a city or ZIP code, or when we look up the town for a set of coordinates, that location text or those coordinates (along with technical information such as the server's IP address and request details) are sent to Nominatim. We do not send your name or email to Nominatim.
  • Google Fonts – the app loads typefaces from fonts.googleapis.com and font files from fonts.gstatic.com. Google receives the usual technical request data, such as your IP address. We do not send your name, email, or messages to Google Fonts.
  • jsDelivr – the app loads the Supabase client library from cdn.jsdelivr.net. jsDelivr receives the usual technical request data, such as your IP address. We do not send your name, email, or messages to jsDelivr.

Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information if we believe in good faith that it is reasonably necessary to: comply with a law, regulation, subpoena, court order, or other legal process; protect the safety of any person, including in connection with a report of violence or a threat of harm; investigate or prevent fraud, abuse, or security issues; or protect the rights or property of Boxing Buddies, our users, or the public.

Business changes. If Boxing Buddies is transferred to a company we form, or is involved in a merger, acquisition, sale of assets, or similar transaction, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will require the recipient to honor this Privacy Policy or give you notice of any material change.

With your direction. We may share information when you ask us to or give us permission.

We do not sell or "share" (for cross-context behavioral advertising) your personal information, as those terms are defined under California and other US state privacy laws.

7. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy:

  • Account and profile data – while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your profile, photo, and location data within 30 days.
  • Swipes, matches, messages, and sessions – while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile is removed and your matches are removed with it. The messages in those matches are deleted with the match. They are not kept on screen as messages from a deleted user.
  • Reports and blocks – we may keep records of reports, blocks, and enforcement actions for up to 2 years after an account is deleted, to protect the community (for example, to prevent someone who was removed for misconduct from simply rejoining).
  • Gym listings – approved gym listings are public information about a business and may remain on the Service after the submitting user deletes their account. They are not linked to your identity publicly.
  • Backups and logs – data in routine backups and server logs maintained by our providers is deleted on their normal schedules, within 30 days.
  • Legal holds – we may keep information longer where required by law or needed to resolve disputes or enforce our Terms.

8. Your choices and controls

  • Edit your profile. You can update or remove your profile information at any time in the app.
  • Location. You can use a typed city or ZIP code instead of GPS, and you can turn off location permission in your browser or device settings.
  • Block users. You can block any user. Blocked users can no longer see you, match with you, or message you.
  • Delete your account. You can delete your account in the app settings or by emailing cyrusjustin06@gmail.com. Deleting your account removes your profile from the Service.
  • Request access or deletion. You can ask us for a copy of your personal information or ask us to delete it by emailing cyrusjustin06@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on the request (for example, by confirming the request from the email address on your account).

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including using established providers (Supabase and Vercel), encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls designed so that users can only read the data they are permitted to see, and passwordless sign-in. However, no system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your information. Please keep access to your email account and devices secure, and tell us right away at cyrusjustin06@gmail.com if you believe your account has been compromised.

If we learn that someone under 18 has created an account, we will delete the account and associated information. If you believe a minor is using the Service, please report the profile in the app or contact us at cyrusjustin06@gmail.com.

11. Your US state privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under state privacy laws, such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act) and similar laws in states including Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon, and others. Some of these laws apply only to businesses that meet certain size or revenue thresholds, and they may not all apply to Boxing Buddies today. Regardless, we aim to honor the following requests from any user in the United States:

  • Right to know / access: ask what personal information we have about you and get a copy.
  • Right to delete: ask us to delete your personal information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Right to correct: ask us to fix inaccurate information.
  • Right to opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising: we do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • Sensitive information: some state laws treat precise geolocation as "sensitive personal information." We use location only to provide the Service (showing nearby partners and approximate distance), and not to infer characteristics about you or for advertising.
  • Non-discrimination: we will not treat you differently for exercising your privacy rights.
  • Authorized agents and appeals: where state law allows, you may use an authorized agent to submit a request, and you may appeal our decision on a request by replying to our response.

California "Shine the Light." We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

Categories of personal information (for California residents). In the past 12 months, we have collected: identifiers (such as email address and account ID); personal records (such as name and photo); geolocation data (approximate and device location); internet or network activity (such as IP address and usage logs); audio/visual information (profile photos); and inferences or characteristics you provide (such as weight class, experience level, and preferences). Sources and purposes are described in Sections 3 and 4, and the categories of recipients are described in Section 6.
